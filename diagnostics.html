Advanced Diagnostics

Accurate diagnosis is the foundation of effective treatment. We utilize the latest imaging and testing technology.

Retina Imaging

OCT Scan

Optical Coherence Tomography (OCT) is a non-invasive imaging test that uses light waves to take cross-section pictures of your retina, crucial for diagnosing glaucoma and macular degeneration.

Cornea Analysis

Corneal Topography

A computer-assisted diagnostic tool that creates a 3D map of the surface curvature of the cornea. Essential for LASIK evaluation and keratoconus diagnosis.

Glaucoma Testing

Visual Field Test

Measures the entire scope of your vision, including peripheral (side) vision. It helps our doctors map vision loss caused by glaucoma or neurological diseases.

Retina Imaging

Fundus Photography

Captures highly detailed photographs of the back of the eye (fundus) to monitor changes in the optic nerve, macula, and retinal blood vessels.

General Checkup

Digital Eye Screening

Comprehensive auto-refraction and digital screening to determine your exact prescription and screen for common ocular abnormalities.

Tonometry

Eye Pressure Test

Fast, painless measurement of intraocular pressure (IOP) using non-contact tonometry to check for signs of glaucoma.
