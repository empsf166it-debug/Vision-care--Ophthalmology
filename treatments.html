Cataract Surgery Journey

Our micro-incision cataract surgery is a painless, 15-minute procedure utilizing premium intraocular lenses to restore crystal clear vision.

1

Condition

Clouding of the eye's natural lens causing blurry vision, glare, and difficulty reading or driving at night.

2

Diagnosis

Comprehensive slit-lamp exam and biometry testing to measure the exact dimensions of your eye for the new lens.

3

Treatment

Phacoemulsification surgery under local anesthesia to remove the cloudy lens and insert a premium artificial lens.

4

Recovery

Fast recovery. Most patients experience improved vision within 24 hours. Eye drops prescribed for 2-4 weeks.

LASIK Laser Vision Correction

Blade-free, custom topography-guided laser surgery designed to safely eliminate your dependence on glasses or contact lenses.

1

Condition

Refractive errors including Myopia (nearsightedness), Hyperopia (farsightedness), and Astigmatism.

2

Diagnosis

Detailed corneal topography, pachymetry (corneal thickness), and wavefront analysis to ensure you are a safe candidate.

3

Treatment

Femtosecond laser creates a micro-flap, followed by an excimer laser to perfectly reshape the cornea in seconds.

4

Recovery

Immediate visual improvement. You can usually return to work and normal activities the very next day.

Glaucoma Management & Surgery

Advanced interventions to lower intraocular pressure and halt optic nerve damage.

1

Condition

Elevated eye pressure causing progressive damage to the optic nerve, leading to irreversible peripheral vision loss.

2

Diagnosis

Tonometry (pressure check), OCT scan of the optic nerve, and visual field testing to assess damage.

3

Treatment

Ranges from medicated eye drops and SLT laser therapy, to Minimally Invasive Glaucoma Surgery (MIGS) or Trabeculectomy.

4

Recovery

Depending on surgery, recovery takes 1-4 weeks with strict follow-ups to monitor eye pressure stability.

Surgery Preparation & Recovery

Before Your Surgery

  • Stop wearing contact lenses as advised.
  • Arrange for transportation post-surgery.
  • Follow fasting guidelines if applicable.

After Your Surgery

  • Use prescribed eye drops regularly.
  • Avoid rubbing or pressing your eyes.
  • Wear protective eyewear provided.
