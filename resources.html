Before Your Appointment

To ensure a smooth and efficient consultation, please prepare the following:


  • Bring your current eyeglasses and contact lenses.
  • Bring a list of all current medications, including eye drops.
  • Bring a valid photo ID and your insurance card.
  • Arrange for a driver, as your eyes may be dilated during the exam, causing temporary blurriness.

Surgery Preparation

If you are scheduled for an eye surgery (such as Cataract or LASIK), follow these guidelines:


  • Stop wearing contact lenses 1-2 weeks before your pre-op measurements, as directed by your doctor.
  • Do not apply makeup, lotions, or perfumes on the day of surgery.
  • Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing.
  • Follow specific fasting guidelines provided by the surgical coordinator if general anesthesia is used.

Post-Surgery Care & Medication

Proper post-operative care is critical for healing. Your specific instructions may vary, but generally:


  • Use prescribed eye drops exactly as scheduled. Do not skip doses.
  • Wear the protective eye shield while sleeping for the first week.
  • Avoid rubbing or pressing on your eye.
  • Avoid strenuous activities, heavy lifting, and swimming for at least 2 weeks.
  • Attend all scheduled follow-up appointments.

Insurance & Payment Information

VisionCare partners with major medical insurance providers. Please note:


  • We accept Medicare, Medicaid, and most private health insurance plans.
  • Co-pays and deductibles are due at the time of service.
  • For elective procedures like LASIK, we offer flexible financing options.
  • Please call our billing department at +1 (800) 123-4567 for specific coverage inquiries.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How long does a comprehensive eye exam take?
A comprehensive exam usually takes between 45 minutes to 1.5 hours, depending on whether your eyes need to be dilated.
Is cataract surgery painful?
No. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia (numbing drops). You may feel mild pressure, but it is not painful.
Can I drive myself home after dilation?
Dilation makes your eyes sensitive to light and blurs near vision for 2-4 hours. We strongly recommend having someone drive you home.
How soon can I return to work after LASIK?
Most patients achieve excellent vision within 24 hours and can return to work the very next day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a referral to book an appointment?

No, you can book an appointment directly. However, if you're using insurance, please check your provider's requirements.

How long does a comprehensive eye exam take?

Please allow 1 to 1.5 hours for a comprehensive exam, as we may need to dilate your pupils.

Will I be able to drive after my appointment?

If your eyes are dilated during the exam, your vision will be blurry and light-sensitive. We strongly recommend having someone drive you home.
